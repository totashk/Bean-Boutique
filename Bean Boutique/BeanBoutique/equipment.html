Brewing Equipment

Our Equipment Range

From beginner to professional, there's a brewer for every level.

Pour-Over

Hario V60 (Ceramic)

The V60 is arguably the most popular pour-over dripper in specialty coffee. Its spiral ridges and large hole give you full control over extraction time, making it great for experimenting with recipes.

Usage tip: Use a 1:16 coffee-to-water ratio, pour in slow circles, and aim for a 3–3.5 minute total brew time.

Pour-Over

Chemex 6-Cup

Equal parts coffee brewer and design object. The Chemex's thick paper filters remove fine particles and oils, producing an exceptionally clean, clear cup. Ideal for light-roast single origins.

Usage tip: Pre-wet the filter with hot water to remove any papery taste. Bloom the grounds for 45 seconds before your main pour.

55.00 LYD
Immersion

AeroPress Original

The AeroPress is almost impossible to brew a bad cup with. It's forgiving, fast (under 2 minutes), and produces a concentrated, full-bodied coffee. Great for travel too.

Usage tip: Try the inverted method for longer steep times. Fine grind gives espresso-style, coarse grind gives filter-style results.

60.00 LYD
Grinder

Comandante C40 MK4

The gold standard of hand grinders. Its precision-engineered steel burrs produce an incredibly consistent grind at any setting. If you're investing in good coffee, this is worth every penny.

Usage tip: Grind fresh before every brew. The nitrogen blade steel keeps edge retention for years.

50.00 LYD
Kettle

Fellow Stagg EKG Kettle

A temperature-controlled gooseneck kettle that looks incredible and works even better. Set your exact temperature and hold it for as long as you need. The narrow spout gives you full control of pour rate.

Usage tip: 93°C for light roasts, 88°C for dark. Pre-heat your dripper with a small pour before brewing.

210.00 LYD
Accessory

Acaia Pearl Scale

Precision weighing is the single biggest upgrade most home brewers can make. The Acaia Pearl has a built-in timer and a flow-rate indicator, making it the favourite tool of professional baristas worldwide.

Usage tip: Weigh both your coffee and your water. Eyeballing either one introduces inconsistency that no technique can fix.
